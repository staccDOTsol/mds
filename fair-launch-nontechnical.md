# Understanding PumpDotFun's "Fair Launch" Platform: A Non-Technical Explanation

## What's Happening?

PumpDotFun claims to be a "fair launch" platform on Solana, promising to solve historical problems with token launches. However, the reality shows a system where sophisticated automated tools and privileged access have created new forms of exploitation. The platform's own statistics reveal that 99.97% of tokens fail within minutes, and less than 100,000 tokens out of millions have ever reached minimum value requirements.

1. **False Promise of Fairness**: The platform claims to be fair, but:
   - Bots buy tokens in the first second

2. **Market Impact**: This creates:
